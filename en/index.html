A house that was once a shop, a drying room and a stable

Can Narcís has been the family home for generations. It dates from 1688 and joins three old houses, all built of stone. The ground floor held the cow and pig stalls, the kitchen and the village's little grocery shop, with a bar and a barber's; hens lived in the courtyard, and the first floor was the family's main living area, with the dining room and bedrooms. The entrance lintel still bears the carved date of construction.

In 2003 we restored it while keeping its essence: the original stone walls, the stone and brick vaults (Catalan vaulting), the wooden beams and, on the first floor, the original hydraulic floor tiles. The furniture belonged to the family, from the early and mid twentieth century, mixed with colonial-style wooden pieces and a few modern ones. And we added everything you need for a comfortable stay: heating, air conditioning, fast wifi and a modern kitchen.

Casavells and the Baix Empordà

Casavells is a small medieval hamlet in the municipality of Corçà, clustered around the Romanesque church of Sant Genís. From here, a short drive takes you to some of the loveliest places in the Empordà.

Historic villages

The capital of ceramics, with pottery shops, antique dealers and a weekly market.

One of the best-preserved medieval villages in Catalonia, with stone streets and a moat carved into the rock.
